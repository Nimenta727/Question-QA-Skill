# 学科专用命题策略 / Subject-specific strategies

Read only the requested subject.

## Mathematics / 数学
Emphasize derivation, assumptions, algebraic equivalence, edge cases, proofs/counterexamples and *why* a method works. Alternate symbolic, verbal and graph interpretation. Check every candidate substitution. Do not confuse calculation slips with lack of conceptual understanding.

## Physics & chemistry / 物理、化学
Measure model selection, unit analysis, conservation laws, experimental control, assumptions, causal explanation and interpreting evidence. Supply constants and measurement uncertainty when needed. No unsafe practical experimental steps.

## Life sciences / 生物
Focus on mechanisms, systems interactions, causal claims vs correlation, data interpretation, variable controls and evidence limitations. Avoid invented definitive findings.

## Language arts / 语文、英语
Distinguish literal comprehension, structural reasoning, inference with textual proof, rhetoric and coherent writing. For open texts, allow alternative defensible interpretations with source evidence. Language conventions and grammatical properties can have exact keys.

## History & civics / 历史、政治
Require historical context, sourced chronology, competing explanations and clear separation of fact from interpretation. Grade argument quality relative to explicit evidence, never ideological allegiance. Avoid flattening controversy to true/false slogans.

## Geography / 地理
Combine spatial patterns, causal mechanisms, scales, map/data reading and human–environment interactions; state geographic region/time and avoid unsupported locality claims.

## Computer science / 计算机
Test invariants, complexity, failure modes, minimal counterexamples, security boundaries, API contracts and debugging hypotheses. Prefer real snippets and testable inputs; avoid grading on unneeded syntax trivia.

## Interdisciplinary / 跨学科
Identify source-specific terminology and prerequisites per domain; use transfer only where both domains' assumptions are stated. Do not let knowledge from outside assigned material dominate scoring.
